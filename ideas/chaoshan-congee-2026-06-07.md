## Region & Culture

### Chaoshan (潮汕)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈtʃaʊˈʃaʊ/
- **Location**: Eastern Guangdong Province, China (广东省东部)
- **Famous for**: Chaoshan cuisine (潮汕菜), known for its freshness, light flavors, and delicate cooking techniques

### Chaoshan Cuisine
- **Rice congee** (潮汕粥) - Breakfast staple, eaten daily
- **Ginger scallion beef** (牛肉火锅) - Famous dish
- **Raw seafood** (生腌) - Marinated in salt and lime
- **Chaozhou beef hotpot** - Quick-cooked beef slices
