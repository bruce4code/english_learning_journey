## Part 1: Racism, Wealth & Position

### Original Text
> "Do you experience racism? I wouldn't be lying if I say no. I'll put it delicately because they don't want, they dare not show us very direct racism because of the position I'm in right now. Maybe five years ago, even then, you would face it. You know, you'd see the people go like what's this Asian person doing here? I think the distribution of wealth has helped as well."

### Key Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning | Example from Text |
|-------------|---------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Racism** | /ˈreɪ.sɪ.zəm/ | 种族歧视；种族主义 | "Do you experience racism?" |
| **Delicately** | /ˈdel.ɪ.kət.li/ | 委婉地；小心翼翼地 | "I'll put it delicately" |
| **Delicate** | /ˈdel.ɪ.kət/ | 微妙的；谨慎的 | Adjective form of delicately |
| **Position** | /pəˈzɪʃ.ən/ | 地位；位置；职位 | "Because of the position I'm in right now" |
| **Distribution of wealth** | /ˌdɪs.trɪˈbjuː.ʃən əv welθ/ | 财富分配 | "The distribution of wealth has helped" |
| **Distribution** | /ˌdɪs.trɪˈbjuː.ʃən/ | 分布；分配；分发 | "Distribution of wealth" |
| **Dare not** | /deər nɒt/ | 不敢 | "They dare not show us very direct racism" |

### Sentence Patterns
- "I wouldn't be lying if I say no." - 如果我说没有，那我就是在撒谎。
- "I'll put it delicately." - 我会委婉地说。
- "The position I'm in right now." - 我目前的地位。
- "I think the distribution of wealth has helped as well." - 我认为财富分配也有帮助。

## Part 2: Discrimination, Harassment & Compassion

### Original Text
> "But unfortunately, it's quite apparent still today when you have in America, when you hear of Asian older women get punched in the face for just walking down the street and just because young girls or boys get harassed and bullied because they're Asians, I don't understand to date why it's still happening. So that's why when a movie like what we've done with Wicked comes out where we encourage people to see it's beautiful to be different because that's how we are all made."

### Key Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning | Example from Text |
|-------------|---------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Apparent** | /əˈpær.ənt/ | 显然的；明显的 | "It's quite apparent still today" |
| **Punch in the face** | /pʌntʃ ɪn ðə feɪs/ | 挥拳打在脸上 | "Get punched in the face" |
| **Harass** | /həˈræs/ or /ˈhær.əs/ | 骚扰；侵扰 | "Young girls or boys get harassed" |
| **Harassment** | /həˈræs.mənt/ | 骚扰（名词） | "Sexual harassment" |
| **Sexual harassment** | /ˈsek.ʃu.əl həˈræs.mənt/ | 性骚扰 | Related phrase |
| **Bully** | /ˈbʊl.i/ | 霸凌；欺负（动词/名词） | "Young boys get bullied" |
| **To date** | /tuː deɪt/ | 至今；到目前为止 | "I don't understand to date why it's still happening" |
| **Wicked** | /ˈwɪk.ɪd/ | 邪恶的；恶毒的 | "Movie like Wicked" |
| **Come out** | /kʌm aʊt/ | 出版；发布；上映 | "When a movie... comes out" |

### Sentence Patterns
- "It's quite apparent still today." - 即使在今天仍然很明显。
- "Get punched in the face." - 被打脸。
- "Walking down the street." - 走在街上。
- "I don't understand to date why..." - 我至今不明白为什么...
- "It's beautiful to be different." - 与众不同是美丽的。

## Part 3: Human Traits, Judgment & Compassion

### Original Text
> "All of us are different and we should not be, I think one of our worst human traits is we're judgmental. We like to judge. We like to be above, I don't know why. We always think that we have to be better and be able to tell the other person or put them down or whatever it is. I think that is something I hope that we all learn that an act of kindness or compassion goes a long, long way in our world and we are in so desperate need of that."

### Key Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning | Example from Text |
|-------------|---------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Traits** | /treɪts/ | 特性；特点 | "One of our worst human traits" |
| **Judge** | /dʒʌdʒ/ | 判断；审判 | "We like to judge" |
| **Judgmental** | /dʒʌdʒˈmen.təl/ | 爱评判的；品头论足的 | "We're judgmental" |
| **Be above** | /bi əˈbʌv/ | 凌驾于；不屑于 | "We like to be above" |
| **Put sb down** | /pʊt ˈsʌm.bə.di daʊn/ | 贬低某人；奚落某人 | "Tell the other person or put them down" |
| **Compassion** | /kəmˈpæʃ.ən/ | 同情；怜悯 | "An act of kindness or compassion" |
| **In desperate need of** | /ɪn ˈdes.pər.ət niːd əv/ | 急需 | "We are in so desperate need of that" |

### Sentence Patterns
- "One of our worst human traits is..." - 我们最糟糕的人性之一...
- "We like to be above." - 我们喜欢凌驾于他人之上。
- "Put them down." - 贬低他们。
- "An act of kindness goes a long way." - 善意的举动意义重大。
- "We are in desperate need of..." - 我们急需...

## Moral Themes

### Key Messages
1. **Racism still exists** - even if it's not always direct.
2. **Wealth distribution** can change social dynamics.
3. **Human judgment** is a common flaw.
4. **Compassion and kindness** are desperately needed.
5. **Being different** is beautiful and natural.
