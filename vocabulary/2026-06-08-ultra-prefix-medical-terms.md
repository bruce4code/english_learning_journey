## 1. "Ultra-" in Ultrasound

## 2. Is "Ultra-" a Synonym for "Super-"?

### Yes, they are similar!

| Prefix | Meaning | Origin | Example |
|--------|---------|--------|---------|
| **Ultra-** | Beyond, extremely, on the far side of | Latin | ultrasound, ultraviolet, ultramodern |
| **Super-** | Above, over, beyond | Latin | superman, supermarket, superpower |

### Key Difference

| Prefix | Connotation | Examples |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Ultra-** | Beyond the normal range; extreme end of a spectrum | ultraviolet (beyond violet), ultrasound (beyond human hearing), ultramarine (beyond the sea) |
| **Super-** | Above average; of higher quality; very good | supermarket (larger than a market), superman (above human), superstar (above star) |

### Comparison Table

| Ultra-word | Meaning | Super-word | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Ultrasound** | Beyond human hearing range | **Super sound** | (not a word) |
| **Ultraviolet** | Beyond violet (invisible) | **Super violet** | (not a word) |
| **Ultramodern** | Extremely modern | **Super modern** | Very modern |
| **Ultrafast** | Extremely fast | **Superfast** | Very fast |

### Simple Memory Aid

- **Ultra-** = "beyond the limit/range" (more scientific)
- **Super-** = "above average, better than" (more common)
