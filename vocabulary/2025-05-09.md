## Today's Words

### 1. resume
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈrez.ə.meɪ/ (noun) /rɪˈzjuːm/ (verb)
- **Part of Speech**:
  - Noun (n.) - a summary of someone's education and work experience
  - Verb (v.) - to begin again or continue after a pause
- **Chinese Meaning**: 名词=简历；动词=重新开始/继续
- **Example Sentences**:
  - Please send your resume to our HR department.
    （请将您的简历发送到我们的人事部。）
  - After a short break, we resumed our discussion.
    （短暂休息后，我们继续讨论。）
- **Synonyms**:
  - Noun: CV, summary, curriculum vitae
  - Verb: continue, restart, proceed
- **Antonyms**: pause, stop, halt
- **Common Collocations**:
  - resume doing something 继续做某事
  - resume one's career 重新开始职业生涯
  - a resume of the meeting 会议摘要
- **Word Formation**: re- (again) + sume (to take)
- **Memory Tip**: re (again) + sume (take) = take again = continue/resume
- **Personal Note**: ⚠️ Watch out! The noun and verb have different pronunciations!
  - resume (noun) = /ˈrez.ə.meɪ/
  - resume (verb) = /rɪˈzjuːm/

### 2. exhibition
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌek.sɪˈbɪʃ.ən/
- **Part of Speech**: Noun (n.)
- **Chinese Meaning**: 展览；展会
- **Example Sentences**:
  - There's a new art exhibition at the museum.
    （博物馆有一个新的艺术展览。）
  - The exhibition showcases works from local artists.
    （这次展览展示了当地艺术家的作品。）
- **Synonyms**: display, show, showcase, presentation
- **Common Collocations**:
  - art exhibition 艺术展
  - exhibition hall 展览厅
  - on exhibition 展出中
- **Word Formation**: exhibit (v.) + -ion (suffix) = exhibition (n.)
- **Related Words**:
  - exhibitor 参展商
  - exhibitive 展览的
- **Memory Tip**: ex (out) + hibit (hold) = hold out to show = exhibition
- **Personal Note**: exhibit vs exhibition
  - exhibit = verb (to show)
  - exhibition = noun (the event itself)

### 3. discipline
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈdɪs.ə.plɪn/
- **Part of Speech**:
  - Noun (n.) - control over one's behavior; a subject of study
  - Verb (v.) - to train someone to obey rules
- **Chinese Meaning**: 名词=纪律/学科；动词=训练/管教
- **Example Sentences**:
  - Discipline is the key to success.
    （纪律是成功的关键。）
  - Mathematics is a challenging discipline.
    （数学是一门具有挑战性的学科。）
  - You need to discipline yourself to study every day.
    （你需要约束自己每天学习。）
- **Synonyms**:
  - Discipline (control): order, control, regulation
  - Discipline (subject): subject, field, branch
  - Discipline (train): train, drill, practice
- **Antonyms**: chaos, disorder
- **Common Collocations**:
  - self-discipline 自律
  - academic discipline 学术科目
  - discipline oneself 自律
- **Memory Tip**: Great word for writing! "Self-discipline" sounds more sophisticated than "self-control"
- **Personal Note**: Perfect for IELTS/TOEFL essays about personal development
