## Core Words Comparison

### 1. Whole
- **Pronunciation**: /həʊl/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 整个的 / 全部的
- **Usage**: Emphasizes completeness, the entire thing as one unit
- **Examples**:
  - "I ate the whole pizza." (我吃了整个披萨)
  - "The whole team is working on this." (整个团队都在做这个)
  - "The whole world is watching." (全世界都在看)
- **Grammar Tips**:
  - Usually with singular nouns: "the whole city"
  - NOT used with plural: ❌ "the whole cities"
  - Use "all of the" instead: ✅ "all of the cities"

### 2. All
- **Pronunciation**: /ɔːl/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 所有的 / 全部的（强调个体总和）
- **Usage**: Emphasizes every individual member or item
- **Examples**:
  - "All students must attend." (所有学生都必须参加)
  - "I ate all the cookies." (我吃了所有饼干)
  - "All systems are operational." (所有系统都在运行)
- **Grammar Tips**:
  - Works with plural nouns: "all people"
  - Works with uncountable: "all water"
  - Can be: all of + noun / all + noun

### 3. Overall
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌəʊvərˈɔːl/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 总体的 / 全面的 / 大致上的
- **Usage**: Taking everything into account, considering all aspects
- **Examples**:
  - "The overall cost is $500." (总费用是500美元)
  - "Overall, the project was successful." (总体来说，项目是成功的)
  - "The overall design looks great." (整体设计看起来很棒)
- **Grammar Tips**:
  - Can use as adjective: "overall score"
  - Can use as adverb: "Overall, I think..."
  - More abstract/implicit than "whole" or "all"

## More Related Words

### 4. Entire
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪnˈtaɪər/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 全部的 / 完整的
- **Similar to**: Whole (slightly more formal)
- **Examples**:
  - "The entire process took 3 hours."
  - "I read the entire book in one day."
  - "The entire staff attended the meeting."
- **Key Difference from Whole**: Slightly more formal; often interchangeable

### 5. Total
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈtəʊtl/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 总的 / 总计的
- **Usage**: Emphasizes sum or complete amount
- **Examples**:
  - "The total amount is $100."
  - "Total failure. We need to start over."
  - "There was total chaos in the office."
- **Grammar Tips**: Often used with numbers, amounts, quantities

### 6. Complete
- **Pronunciation**: /kəmˈpliːt/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 完全的 / 完整的
- **Usage**: Nothing missing, finished, whole
- **Examples**:
  - "This is a complete list of requirements."
  - "We need a complete overhaul of the system."
  - "The project is now complete."

### 7. Entirety
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪnˈtaɪərəti/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 整体 / 全部（名词形式）
- **Examples**:
  - "We analyzed the data in its entirety."
  - "The entirety of the report is available."

### 8. Wholesome
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈhəʊlsəm/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 健康的 / 有益的（注意：不是"整个"的意思！）
- **Examples**:
  - "This is a wholesome meal."
  - "It's a wholesome family movie."
- **⚠️ Warning**: This word means "healthy/beneficial", NOT "full of whole"!

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Wrong
- "The whole people" (whole with plural)
- "All of whole" (double meaning)

### ✅ Correct
- "All people" or "the whole population"
- "All of us" or "the whole group"

---

## Usage by Context

### In Business/Work
- **Overall**: "Overall performance" / "Overall strategy"
- **Entire**: "The entire company" / "Entire project lifecycle"
- **Total**: "Total revenue" / "Total headcount"
- **Whole**: "The whole department" / "Whole package"

### In Daily Life
- **All**: "All my friends" / "All day"
- **Whole**: "The whole day" / "Whole family"
- **Complete**: "Complete mess" / "Complete surprise"

### In Technology
- **All**: "All systems go" / "All users"
- **Overall**: "Overall architecture" / "Overall system design"
- **Total**: "Total memory" / "Total capacity"
