**Pronunciation**: /teɪl/

**Meaning**: 尾巴 / 尾部 / 跟踪

## Word Family

### 1. Tail (n./v.)
- **Pronunciation**: /teɪl/
- **Meaning(n)**: 尾巴
- **Meaning(v)**: 跟踪
- **Example**: "The dog wagged its tail."

### 2. Tailcost (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪl.kɒst/
- **Meaning**: 尾部成本 / 后续成本
- **Usage**: A business term referring to costs incurred at the tail end of a project or process
- **Example**: "We need to account for tailcost in the budget."

### 3. Tailcoat (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪl.kəʊt/
- **Meaning**: 燕尾服
- **Example**: "He wore a tailcoat to the ball."

### 4. Tailpipe (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪl.paɪp/
- **Meaning**: 排气管
- **Example**: "The car's tailpipe was damaged."

### 5. Cocktail (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈkɒk.teɪl/
- **Meaning**: 鸡尾酒
- **Example**: "We ordered cocktails at the bar."

### 6. Detail (n./v.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈdiː.teɪl/ (n.) /dɪˈteɪl/ (v.)
- **Meaning**: 细节 / 详细说明
- **Root**: "de-" (down) + "tail" (cut)
- **Etymology**: Originally "cut into pieces"
- **Example**: "Please provide more details."

### 7. Retail (n./v.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈriː.teɪl/
- **Meaning**: 零售
- **Root**: "re-" (again) + "tail" (cut)
- **Etymology**: "To cut again" → "to sell in small quantities"
- **Example**: "The retail price is $50."

### 8. Tailor (n./v.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪ.lər/
- **Meaning**: 裁缝/定制
- **Root**: "tail" + "-or" (person who)
- **Etymology**: Originally "one who cuts cloth"
- **Example**: "He had his suit tailored."

### 9. Tailer (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪ.lər/
- **Meaning**: 尾随者/跟踪者
- **Note**: Same pronunciation as "tailor", different meaning
- **Warning**: Often confused with "tailor"!
- **Example**: "The tailer followed the suspect."

### 10. Curtail (v.)
- **Pronunciation**: /kɜːˈteɪl/
- **Meaning**: 缩减/削减
- **Root**: "cur-" (short) + "tail" (cut)
- **Etymology**: "To cut short"
- **Example**: "We need to curtail expenses."

### 11. Entail (v.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɪnˈteɪl/
- **Meaning**: 需要/牵涉/必然导致
- **Root**: "en-" (make) + "tail" (cut/limit)
- **Etymology**: "To impose or involve"
- **Example**: "This job entails a lot of traveling."

### 12. Tailwind (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪl.wɪnd/
- **Meaning**: 顺风
- **Meaning**: Wind blowing from behind, helping you go faster
- **Example**: "The tailwind helped us arrive early."

### 13. Tailspin (n.)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈteɪl.spɪn/
- **Meaning**: 尾旋/混乱状态
- **Meaning**: A situation where everything goes wrong; a state of chaos
- **Example**: "The market went into a tailspin."

## Root Analysis: "Tail" = "Cut"

The original Latin root **"tail"** means **"to cut"**.

| Word | Latin Root | Literal Meaning | Modern Meaning |
|------|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Detail** | de- + tail | cut down | specific piece of information |
| **Retail** | re- + tail | cut again | sell in small pieces |
| **Tailor** | tail + -or | one who cuts | person who cuts cloth |
| **Curtail** | cur- + tail | cut short | reduce, shorten |
| **Entail** | en- + tail | cut in | involve, require |
