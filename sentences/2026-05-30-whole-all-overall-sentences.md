# Whole / All / Overall - Sentence Examples

## Date
2026-05-30

## Category
Sentences - Vocabulary in Context

---

## Technical Sentences

### Whole
- "We need to refactor the whole codebase."
- "The whole system went down during maintenance."
- "The whole team is responsible for code quality."
- "I spent the whole day debugging this issue."

### All
- "All tests passed before the deployment."
- "All developers must review the pull request."
- "Please fix all the bugs in this module."
- "All API endpoints are responding correctly."

### Overall
- "Overall, the architecture is well-designed."
- "The overall response time improved by 40%."
- "Overall system performance meets our requirements."
- "The overall cost of cloud infrastructure increased."

## Daily Conversation Sentences

### Whole
- "I ate the whole cake by myself."
- "The whole family went on vacation."
- "She spent the whole night studying."
- "The whole story was a lie."

### All
- "All my friends came to the party."
- "I gave it my all."
- "All we need is love."
- "She knows all the answers."

### Overall
- "Overall, I think we had a great trip."
- "The overall experience was positive."
- "Overall, the food was delicious."
- "Overall, it was a good decision."

## Comparison Sentences

### Whole vs All
- **Whole**: "The whole team agreed." (team as one unit)
- **All**: "All team members agreed." (each individual member)
- **Whole**: "I read the whole book." (entire book as one)
- **All**: "I read all the books." (every single book)

### Overall vs Whole
- **Overall**: "Overall, the event was successful." (considering everything)
- **Whole**: "The whole event was a disaster." (the entire thing)
- **Overall**: "Overall performance improved." (in general)
- **Whole**: "The whole performance was amazing." (from start to finish)

---

## Common Mistake Sentences

### ❌ Incorrect
- "I ate whold pizza." (whold is not a word)
- "The whole peoples are happy." (whole + plural)
- "Overall the team is here." (use "all" or "whole")

### ✅ Correct
- "I ate the whole pizza."
- "All people are happy." or "The whole population is happy."
- "All the team members are here." or "The whole team is here."
