## 今日句子

### 1. 经济话题表达
> "When money is tight, families often opt for cheaper, calorie-dense foods."
- **中文意思**: 当资金紧张时，家庭通常会选择更便宜、高热量的食物。
- **使用场景**: 描述经济压力对生活选择的影响
- **结构分析**: When + 主语 + 动词... → 当...时，主语会...
- **替换练习**:
  - When money is tight, people tend to save more.
  - When time is tight, we need to prioritize.
- **个人备注**: "opt for" 比 "choose" 更正式

### 2. 权衡表达
> "The trade-off between nutrition and affordability."
- **中文意思**: 营养和价格之间的权衡。
- **使用场景**: 讨论两难选择、得失取舍
- **结构分析**: The trade-off between A and B → A和B之间的权衡
- **替换练习**:
  - The trade-off between work and life balance.
  - The trade-off between quality and quantity.
- **个人备注**: 写作中非常好用的表达！

### 3. 表达相关性
> "Body shape has become an unexpected indicator of economic status."
- **中文意思**: 体型已成为经济状况的一个意外指标。
- **使用场景**: 描述某种现象反映另一个现象
- **结构分析**: A has become a/an + 形容词 + indicator of B
- **替换练习**:
  - Social media has become a key indicator of popularity.
  - Test scores have become an indicator of academic ability.
- **个人备注**: indicator 可以替换为 marker, sign, signal

### 4. 表达因果关联
> "There's a strong correlation between socioeconomic status and health outcomes."
- **中文意思**: 社会经济地位与健康状况之间存在很强的相关性。
- **使用场景**: 学术写作、报告分析
- **结构分析**: There's a correlation between A and B
- **替换练习**:
  - There's a correlation between education and income.
  - There's a correlation between exercise and mental health.
- **个人备注**: correlation ≠ causation（相关不等于因果）

### 5. 表达经济衰退影响
> "Economic downturns often lead to changes in lifestyle choices."
- **中文意思**: 经济衰退通常导致生活方式的改变。
- **使用场景**: 经济类话题讨论
- **结构分析**: A often leads to B → A通常导致B
- **替换练习**:
  - Climate change often leads to extreme weather.
  - Technology advances often lead to job changes.
- **个人备注**: 比 "cause" 更委婉
