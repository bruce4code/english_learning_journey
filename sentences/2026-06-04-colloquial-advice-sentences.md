## Key Phrases & Slang

### 1. Keep doing your thing
**Meaning**: Continue with what you're doing; stay focused on your unique approach

**Similar expressions**:
- Keep at it
- Stay in your lane
- Keep doing what you're doing
- Keep it up
- Keep up the good work
- Keep your head down and grind

**Example sentences**:
- "You're making great progress, keep doing your thing!"
- "Just keep doing your thing, and you'll get there."
- "Everyone else might not get it, but you keep doing your thing."

---

### 2. It's gonna get you places
**Meaning**: It will lead to success or good results; it will help you achieve your goals

**Similar expressions**:
- It'll take you places
- It'll get you ahead
- It's gonna open doors for you
- It's gonna lead to good things
- It's gonna help you go far

**Example sentences**:
- "Your hard work is gonna get you places someday."
- "Learning these skills will get you places in your career."
- "That attitude will get you places."

---

### 3. Badass
**Meaning**: Tough, cool, impressive, skilled

**Note**: This is informal slang, use in casual conversations only

**Definition**: Someone who is tough, skilled, impressive, fearless, cool

**Example sentences**:
- "She's a total badass, she never gives up."
- "That's a badass move!"
- "That car is so badass!"
- "You were pretty badass when you were young."

**Note**: This word can be rude in some contexts, use carefully

**Other terms that are safer**:
- Awesome
- Cool
- Impressive
- Amazing

## Advice for Someone Starting Out

> "What piece of advice do you give?"

**Common advice phrases**:
- "Start small, be consistent."
- "Don't compare your chapter 1 to someone else's chapter 20."
- "Focus on the process, not just the outcome."
- "Fail fast, learn faster."
- "Small daily habits compound over time."
- "Stay curious."
- "You don't need all the answers, just keep learning."
- "The best time to start was 5 years ago, the second best time is now."
- "Enjoy the journey, not just the destination."

## Example Responses
