## 1. "In the grand scheme of things"

### Meaning
Considered from a wide or long-term perspective; in the overall view of life/the world/situations.

### Similar Expressions
- In the big picture
- Looking at the big picture
- In the overall scheme of things
- In the larger scheme of things

## 2. Plan vs Scheme

### Plan (Neutral/Positive)
- **Meaning**: A detailed proposal for doing or achieving something
- **Connotation**: Neutral, sometimes positive (organized)
- **Common uses**: Project plan, business plan, life plan, travel plan
- **Examples**:
  - "I made a plan to visit China next year."
  - "Let's make a plan for the meeting."
  - "She has a 5-year career plan."

### Scheme (Often Negative)
- **Meaning**: A systematic plan for doing something, usually devious, dishonest or secretive
- **Connotation**: Often negative, implies sneaky or dishonest intent
- **Common uses**: Get-rich-quick scheme, scam scheme, devious scheme, evil scheme
- **Examples**:
  - "He came up with a scheme to steal money."
  - "It was a scheme to cheat people out of their savings."
  - "The company was involved in a tax evasion scheme."

### Quick Comparison

| Word | Connotation | Common Examples |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Plan** | Neutral/Positive | Business plan, vacation plan, 5-year plan |
| **Scheme** | Often Negative | Scam, devious scheme, get-rich-quick scheme |

## 2b. Scheme vs Schema

### Scheme
- **Pronunciation**: /skiːm/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 计划；阴谋；方案
- **Common uses**:
  - "The government introduced a new housing scheme."
  - "It was just another get-rich-quick scheme."
  - "They worked out a scheme to escape."
- **Connotation**: Often neutral or negative

### Schema
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈskiːmə/
- **Chinese Meaning**: 架构；模式；图解（认知科学中的“图式”）
- **Common uses**:
  - In **cognitive science**: A mental framework for organizing information and understanding
  - In **database**: A structure that defines how data is organized
  - In **XML/JSON**: A specification for data format
- **Connotation**: Formal, technical
- **Examples**:
  - "Children develop schemas for understanding the world."
  - "The database schema defines the tables and their relationships."
  - "We need to update the XML schema."

### Quick Comparison (Scheme vs Schema)

| Word | Pronunciation | Meaning & Context |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Scheme** | /skiːm/ | Plan (often secretive or negative); everyday English |
| **Schema** | /ˈskiːmə/ | Mental model; database/data structure; technical |

### "Schema" in Tech/Programming
- **Database schema**: Defines tables, columns, relationships
- **JSON schema**: Validates JSON data structure
- **XML schema**: Defines XML document structure
- **Cognitive schema**: Mental model (psychological term)

## 3. Why "scheme" is pronounced /skiːm/ (ch = /k/)

### The Rule: Greek Origin Words with "ch" = /k/

Many English words with "ch" that come from **Greek roots** pronounce the "ch" as /k/ instead of /tʃ/.

### "Scheme" Etymology
- From **Greek**: *skhēma* (σχῆμα) = form, shape, plan
- Through Latin and French, it became "scheme" in English
- The "ch" in Greek origin words is pronounced /k/

### Greek Origin "ch" → /k/ Words

| Word | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|------|---------------|---------|
| **scheme** | /skiːm/ | plan (often secretive) |
| **school** | /skuːl/ | place of learning |
| **schedule** | /ˈskedʒ.uːl/ (US) or /ˈʃed.juːl/ (UK) | timetable |
| **chorus** | /ˈkɔːrəs/ | group of singers |
| **chemistry** | /ˈkemɪstri/ | science of matter |
| **chronic** | /ˈkrɒnɪk/ | long-lasting |
| **character** | /ˈkærəktər/ | personality, letter |
| **chaos** /ˈkeɪɒs/ | total disorder |
| **school** /skuːl/ | place of education |

### Latin/French Origin "ch" → /tʃ/ Words

| Word | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|------|---------------|---------|
| **church** | /tʃɜːrtʃ/ | place of worship |
| **chair** /tʃer/ | seat with back |
| **change** /tʃeɪndʒ/ | become different |
| **child** /tʃaɪld/ | young person |
| **check** /tʃek/ | examine |

### Quick Memory Aide
- Greek origin? → **ch = /k/**
- Latin/French origin? → **ch = /tʃ/**
