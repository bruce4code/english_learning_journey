## Two Major CC Pronunciation Rules

### 1. CC = /k/ (Most Common)
**When to use this rule:**
- Before vowels: a, o, u
- Before consonants
- In stressed closed syllables

**Example Words:**
- **access** /ˈæk ses/
- **occur** /əˈkɜːr/
- **success** /səkˈses/
- **account** /əˈkaʊnt/
- **accomplish** /əˈkʌmplɪʃ/
- **accuse** /əˈkjuːz/
- **accidental** /ˌæksɪˈdentl/

---

### 2. CC = /ks/
**When to use this rule:**
- Before vowels: e, i

**Example Words:**
- **accept** /əkˈsept/
- **accident** /ˈæksɪdənt/
- **access** /ˈæk ses/
- **accessory** /ækˈsesəri/
- **acceptance** /əkˈseptəns/
- **accelerate** /əkˈseləreɪt/

---

## The Accuracy Example

### accuracy /ˈækjərəsi/

**Word Breakdown:** ac-cu-ra-cy

1. **ac** → /æk/
   - The first 'c' in 'cc' is followed by 'a'
   - So it makes the /k/ sound

2. **cu** → /jə/
   - The second 'c' is followed by 'u'
   - Combines with 'u' to make /jə/ sound
   - Doesn't make a separate /k/ sound

**Simple Memory Aid:**
✅ CC before 'a' → pronounces /k/
✅ CC before 'e/i' → pronounces /ks/
