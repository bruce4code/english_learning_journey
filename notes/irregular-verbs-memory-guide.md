## Topic
Irregular Verbs: Patterns & Memory Tricks

## Why Are They Called "Irregular"?

These verbs don't follow the normal **-ed** rule for past tense. But guess what? **They DO follow patterns!** Recognizing patterns makes memorization 10x easier.

---

## Part 1: The 4 Types (Easy Classification)

### Type 1: AAA (All Same)
**Rule**: Base = Past = Past Participle  
**Example**: `cut → cut → cut`

| Base | Past | PP | Meaning |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| cost | cost | cost | 花费 |
| cut | cut | cut | 切 |
| hit | hit | hit | 打 |
| hurt | hurt | hurt | 伤害 |
| let | let | let | 让 |
| put | put | put | 放 |
| **read** | **read** | **read** | 阅读 (pronunciation: /riːd/ → /red/) |
| set | set | set | 设置 |
| shut | shut | shut | 关闭 |

### 💡 Memory Trick for AAA
> **CUT-HIT-HURT-LET-PUT-SHUT-SET**
> Just add **-ed** in your mind: "I cut, I did cut, I have cut"  
> Think: "What you do, you did, you have done it."

---

### Type 2: AAB (Rare)
**Rule**: Past = Past Participle  
**Example**: `beat → beat → beaten`

| Base | Past | PP | Meaning |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| beat | beat | **beaten** | 打败 |

### 💡 Memory Trick for AAB
> **BEAT** has a "T" at the end → needs **-en** to stay strong!  
> Think: "You beat them, you beat them, you've beaten them!"

---

### Type 3: ABA (Past = Base)
**Rule**: Past = Base  
**Example**: `come → came → come`

| Base | Past | PP | Meaning |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| become | became | become | 变成 |
| come | came | **come** | 来 |
| run | ran | **run** | 跑 |

### 💡 Memory Trick for ABA
> **COME** and **RUN** → Both end with **-ome/-un**  
> Past is **came/ran** → Back to home base!  
> Think: "What comes, came back. What runs, ran back."

### Type 4: ABB (Most Common!)
**Rule**: Past = Past Participle (but different from Base)

#### 4A: Vowel Changes (DIPHTHONG CHANGES)

| Base | Past | PP | Meaning | Memory |
|------|------|-----|---------|--------|
| meet | met | met | 遇见 | MEET → MET (ee→e) |
| feed | fed | fed | 喂养 | FEED → FED |
| feel | felt | felt | 感觉 | FEEL → FElt |
| sleep | slept | slept | 睡觉 | SLEEP → SLEPT |
| sweep | swept | swept | 打扫 | SWEEP → SWEPT |
| keep | kept | kept | 保持 | KEEP → KEPT |
| leave | left | left | 离开 | LEAVE → LEFT |
| mean | meant | meant | 意味 | MEAN → MEANT |

### 💡 Memory Trick for Vowel Changes
> **"Big vowels go small in the past"**  
> EE → E (meet-met, feel-felt, sleep-selt)  
> Think: Vowels "shrink" when talking about the past!

#### 4B: -ought/-aught Endings

| Base | Past | PP | Meaning | Memory |
|------|------|-----|---------|--------|
| buy | **bought** | bought | 买 | BUY → BOUGHT |
| bring | **brought** | brought | 带来 | BRING → BROUGHT |
| fight | **fought** | fought | 战斗 | FIGHT → FOUGHT |
| think | **thought** | thought | 思考 | THINK → THOUGHT |
| catch | **caught** | caught | 抓住 | CATCH → CAUGHT |
| teach | **taught** | taught | 教 | TEACH → TAUGHT |

### 💡 Memory Trick for -ought/-aught
> **BOUGHT, BROUGHT, THOUGHT, FOUGHT, CAUGHT, TAUGHT**  
> All rhyme with "T"!  
> **BOUGHT** rhymes with "thought" → both about the past (buying/thinking)

## Part 3: Memory Tricks Collection

### Trick 1: The "Story" Method
Create a sentence with all three forms:

> **"I **saw** the bird **fly**, it **flew** away, and has **flown** to the sky."**

### Trick 2: Sound Association
- **Ring**: RING → RANG → RUNG (Ring rhymes with thing!)
- **Sing**: SING → SANG → SUNG (Similar pattern)
- **Drink**: DRINK → DRANK → DRUNK (INK → ANK → UNK)

### Trick 3: Visual Memory
- **Give-Gave-Given**: Think of "I give, I gave, I have given love"
- **Break-Broke-Broken**: Picture a "broken" glass

### Trick 4: Word Family
Group by past tense sound:

| Sound | Verbs |
|-------|-------|
| /ɛt/ (et) | get, forget, set, let, met, sleep, sweep |
| /ɔːt/ (ought) | bought, brought, fought, thought, caught, taught |
| /ɪd/ (id) | build, found, ground, wound |

## Part 4: Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Fill in the blanks
1. I have _____ the movie three times. (see)
2. Yesterday, I _____ to the store. (go)
3. She has _____ her keys. (lose)
4. He _____ English very well. (speak)

**Answers**: 1. seen 2. went 3. lost 4. speaks

### Exercise 2: Choose the correct form
1. I have _____ (break/broke/broken) the window.
2. She _____ (come/came/come) yesterday.
3. They have _____ (write/wrote/written) the letter.

**Answers**: 1. broken 2. came 3. written
