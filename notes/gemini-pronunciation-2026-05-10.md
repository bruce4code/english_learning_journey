## Part 1: Google Gemini Pronunciation

### Gemini
- **Spelling**: G-E-M-I-N-I
- **Correct Pronunciation**: /ˈdʒem.ə.naɪ/  (JEM-uh-nai)
- **Breaking it down**:
  - GE = /dʒ/ (like "gem")
  - mi = /mɪ/ (like "me")
  - ni = /naɪ/ (like "knee" with long "i")

### Related Tech Product Pronunciations

| Product | Spelling | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|----------|---------------|-------|
| **Gemini** | Gemini | /ˈdʒem.ə.naɪ/ | Google's AI model |
| **Bard** | Bard | /bɑːrd/ | Google's previous AI (rhymes with "card") |
| **Duet AI** | Duet AI | /djuːˈeɪ/ + /eɪ/ | Google's collaboration AI |
| **Copilot** | Copilot | /ˈkəʊ.paɪ.lət/ | Microsoft's AI assistant |
| **ChatGPT** | Chat GPT | /tʃæt/ + /dʒiː piː tiː/ | OpenAI's chatbot |

### Memory Tip
> **GEM-in-i** = "Gem" + "uh" + "knee"  
> Think: "A precious gem with a knee" 🦵💎

## Part 2: -NI Syllable Pronunciation Rules

### Rule 1: -NI at the end of words
When "ni" appears at the end of a word, it's usually pronounced **/naɪ/**

| Word | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Gemini | /ˈdʒem.ə.naɪ/ | Google's AI |
| Toni | /ˈtəʊ.naɪ/ | A name |
| Genie | /ˈdʒiː.naɪ/ | Magical spirit |
|arani | /ˌær.əˈnaɪ/ | A spider (in Japanese: arachnid) |

### Rule 2: -NI in the middle of words
In the middle of words, "ni" can be:
- **/nɪ/** - short i sound
- **/naɪ/** - long i sound

| Word | Pronunciation | Position | Meaning |
|------|--------------|----------|---------|
|香蕉 | banana /bəˈnæn.ə/ | middle | (na = /nə/) |
| 观点 | opinion /əˈpɪn.jən/ | middle | (ion = /jən/) |
| 知识 | knowledge /ˈnɒl.ɪdʒ/ | middle | (kn = silent!) |
| 年轻 | young /jʌŋ/ | middle | - |

### Rule 3: -NI in Latin/Greek words
Many tech words come from Latin where "ni" often represents /naɪ/:

| Word | Pronunciation | Origin | Meaning |
|------|--------------|--------|---------|
| senior | /ˈsiː.ni.ər/ | Latin | 高级的 |
| junior | /ˈdʒuː.ni.ər/ | Latin | 初级的 |
| terminal | /ˈtɜː.mɪ.nəl/ | Latin | 终端 |
| infrastructure | /ˈɪn.frə.strʌk.tʃər/ | Latin | 基础设施 |

### Rule 4: -NI as a suffix
Common -ni suffix patterns:

| Suffix | Pronunciation | Words |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| -ini | /ɪni/ or /aɪni/ | Martini, genie,ellini |
| -oni | /əʊni/ | Spaghetti, macaroni, pepperoni |
| -uni | /juːni/ | Universe, united, university |

## Part 4: Common Mistakes to Avoid

⚠️ **Mistake 1**: Pronouncing "Gemini" as "GEM-uh-nee"
- ❌ Wrong: /ˈdʒem.ə.ni/
- ✅ Correct: /ˈdʒem.ə.naɪ/

⚠️ **Mistake 2**: Pronouncing "banana" with emphasis on "na"
- ❌ Wrong: ba-**NA**-na
- ✅ Correct: **BA**-na-na (stress on first syllable)

⚠️ **Mistake 3**: Forgetting silent letters in "knowledge"
- ❌ Wrong: KNO-wledge
- ✅ Correct: **NOL**-edge (K is silent!)
