## Topic
Pronunciation Rules: EX Letter Combination

## Content

### 1. EX at the Beginning of Words

#### A. EX + Vowel (EX + 元音字母开头)
**Pronunciation**: /ɪɡz/ or /eɡz/

**Examples**:
- exact /ɪɡˈzækt/ - exact
- exam /ɪɡˈzæm/ - exam
- example /ɪɡˈzæmpəl/ - example
- exist /ɪɡˈzɪst/ - exist
- exhibit /ɪɡˈzɪbɪt/ - exhibit
- exhibition /ˌeksɪˈbɪʃən/ - exhibition

#### B. EX + Consonant (EX + 辅音字母开头)
**Pronunciation**: /ɪks/

**Examples**:
- expect /ɪkˈspekt/ - expect
- experience /ɪkˈspɪəriəns/ - experience
- explain /ɪkˈspleɪn/ - explain
- express /ɪkˈspres/ - express
- explore /ɪkˈsplɔː/ - explore
- explode /ɪkˈspləʊd/ - explode

### 2. EX in the Middle or End of Words

#### A. In the Middle of Words
**Pronunciation**: Usually /ɪks/ or /ɪɡz/
- next /nekst/ - next
- text /tekst/ - text
- excuse /ɪkˈskjuːz/ - excuse

#### B. At the End (-ex)
**Pronunciation**: Usually /ɪks/
- box /bɒks/ - box
- fox /fɒks/ - fox
- mix /mɪks/ - mix

### 3. Special Cases

#### A. After Stressed Prefixes
When the prefix is stressed separately:
- re-export /riːˈekspɔːt/ - re-export
- re-examine /riːɪɡˈzæmɪn/ - re-examine

#### B. Common Exceptions
- exit /ˈeksɪt/ - exit (EX = /eks/)
- executive /ɪɡˈzekjʊtɪv/ - executive (EX = /ɪɡz/)

### 4. Memory Tips

#### Rule: "Look at What Follows EX"
- If followed by a **vowel** → /ɪɡz/
- If followed by a **consonant** → /ɪks/

#### Mnemonics:
1. **"Example"** = Ex +ample → Remember: EX + vowel = /ɪɡz/
2. **"Expect"** = Ex + pect → Remember: EX + consonant = /ɪks/

### 5. Practice Exercises

**Identify the pronunciation of EX in these words**:

1. expose (暴露)
2. extra (额外的)
3. export (出口)
4. expression (表达)
5. expensive (昂贵的)

**Answers**:
1. /ɪkspəʊz/ → /ɪks/
2. /ˈekstrə/ → /ɪks/
3. /ˈekspɔːt/ → /ɪks/
4. /ɪkspreʃən/ → /ɪks/
5. /ɪkspensɪv/ → /ɪks/

### 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

⚠️ **Mistake 1**: Confusing "exhibit" and "exhibition"
- exhibit /ɪɡˈzɪbɪt/ → Verb
- exhibition /ˌeksɪˈbɪʃən/ → Noun

⚠️ **Mistake 2**: Mishearing /ɪɡz/ as /eɡz/
- Key point: The /ɪ/ sound should be short and light

⚠️ **Mistake 3**: Assuming all words with EX are pronounced the same
- Remember: Context matters! Always check the following letter.
